# Art sources for `content/tiles/`

## Little Bits Office tileset — **used**

All art in this directory comes from the "Little Bits Office tileset" pack supplied
by the project owner (`Little_Bits_Office_tileset.rar` / `.zip` in `~/Downloads`).

| File | Origin |
|---|---|
| `office-floors.png` | `Office tiles/Little_Bits_Office_Floors.png`, **unmodified** |
| `office-walls.png` | `Office tiles/Little_Bits_office_walls.png`, **unmodified** |
| `props/*.png` | sub-rectangles cropped out of the objects/walls sheets (see below) |

The two grid sheets are byte-for-byte copies of the pack originals. The 28 files in
`props/` are lossless crops — the source sheets are sprite collages, not tile grids,
so each prop had to be cut out to become a Tiled image-collection tile.

The pack also ships `businessman1/` character sprites (idle + walk, 4 directions,
16x32 frames). They are **not** used by `office.tmj` but suit the player avatar.

> ⚠️ **License unconfirmed.** This pack shipped with **no license file** and no author
> or URL. Before committing this art or shipping it publicly, confirm the terms with
> wherever it was obtained. Everything here is trivially regenerable from the source
> archive, so it can be removed without touching `office.tmj`'s structure.

## Not used

- **`officeInteriorAssets.zip`** — Office interior assets pack © 2025 Camille Gicquel,
  **CC BY 4.0** (attribution required if ever used). Excluded on technical grounds:
  hand-drawn 3/4-perspective illustrations at arbitrary sizes (171x368, 109x120, …)
  with no tile grid. Wrong projection and ~7x the scale of a 16px top-down map, and
  visually incompatible with the pixel art.
- **`demo_v1.zip`** — not a tileset. `tilesheet.png` is a 600x448 **store preview
  image** with a title banner and labelled room thumbnails; 600 is not a multiple of
  16. Its `tile_manifest.json` ("977 tiles classified") is auto-generated by slicing
  that banner on a 16px grid and labelling cells by opaque-pixel ratio — e.g. tile
  `src_1_0` is typed `corner_BR` but is part of the letter "O" in the title.
